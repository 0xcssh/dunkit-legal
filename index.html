Getting a measurement

  • Film straight on, with your whole body in frame from before the takeoff until after the landing.
  • Keep the phone still — on a tripod or propped up.
  • Good light helps body tracking. If a clip can't be measured, the app tells you why.

Subscription

Manage or cancel Dunk It Pro in your App Store account settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions). To restore a purchase on a new device, use “Restore Purchases” on the subscription screen.
