Dunk It — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

Your jump videos never leave your iPhone.

Dunk It is a vertical-jump training app. This policy explains what the app does with your information. Short version: your profile, your videos and your training history stay on your device; the only things that ever leave it are the ones listed in the table below.

What stays on your device

  • Jump videos. Clips you film or pick from your library are analysed on your iPhone. The body-tracking that measures your jump (Google ML Kit pose detection) runs entirely on the device; frames are never uploaded. Saved clips and their thumbnails are stored in the app's own storage on your device.
  • Your athlete profile — the answers you give during setup (goals, experience, position, training days and location, height, weight, age, dunk hand, standing reach) — and your workout and jump history are stored only on your device.
  • Camera, microphone and photo library access is used only to record or pick a jump clip, and to save a clip when you choose to. The microphone is required by iOS for video capture; the audio is not used.

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

What leaves your device

DataSent toWhy
If — and only if — you choose a leaderboard name: that name, your best measured vertical, your height, the date of that jump, and a random anonymous account identifier. No video, no image, no email. Supabase (our database host) To show your best jump on the global leaderboard. Without a leaderboard name nothing is published.
Purchase and subscription status, an anonymous app user identifier, device and app version information. Apple and RevenueCat (subscription management) To sell, restore and verify your Dunk It Pro subscription. Payment details are handled by Apple; we never see them.
Technical metrics about the on-device pose-detection component (performance, usage, device model) — never your images. Google (ML Kit) Required by Google's ML Kit SDK to deliver and maintain its on-device models.

What we don't do

No advertising, no ad tracking, no selling or sharing of data with data brokers, no tracking across other companies' apps or websites. Dunk It has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email or phone number.

Children

Dunk It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices and deletion

To remove your leaderboard entry, or for any privacy request, email us at the address below with your leaderboard name; we delete the entry within 30 days. Everything else can be removed by deleting the app. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store account settings.
